# Product constitution

The controlling brief is [MISSION_EVIDENCE_FIRST.md](MISSION_EVIDENCE_FIRST.md). This is an independent, unendorsed creator gift under development. Creator permission, source rights, editorial review, and technical readiness are separate gates.

## Identity and voice

Itachi's Corner centers da'wah, learning, and thoughtful dialogue. Its voice is respectful, curious, and lightly playful. It never sensationalizes disagreements, assigns spiritual worth, declares debate victories, or invents endorsements, outcomes, testimonials, or audiences.

The application does not broadcast or host livestreams. External platforms own discovery and viewing. There is no live banner, event countdown, attendance inference, or prominent watch-live action.

## Navigation and narrative

Primary destinations are Home, Explore, Learn, Practice, and Community. My Corner is secondary account navigation. About, Library, Session Notes, Support, and trust information belong in contextual or footer links. Legacy paths redirect without presenting legacy demo content.

The narrative is: Meet Itachi → Explore a real question → Learn from sourced material → Practice thoughtfully → Keep your progress. Each fold has one clear idea and locally dominant action; distinct contextual journeys may appear throughout a substantive page. The hero has one dominant action and at most one relevant secondary action. Do not chase a fixed total CTA count or remove useful interaction to achieve one. A visitor should understand the purpose and readiness of a screen within about five seconds. Empty collections remain empty, with a useful explanation of what will make them available.

## Content truth

No production page uses fictional videos, sample questions, simulated members, invented dates, scores, credentials, stories, mentor availability, course outlines, or book text. Pending evidence is a real product state. Demo labels do not excuse fabricated product content. Test fixtures stay outside public rendering.

An Itachi-derived teaching claim requires the original video URL/ID, attributed speaker, exact original transcript span, half-open millisecond interval, extraction version, confidence, corroboration across videos, reviewer decision/date, and creator authorization. Discovery precedes curriculum. Curriculum and book authoring remain blocked until the controlling brief's Gates 2–4 pass.

Public language describes questions, sources, conversations, and learning. Internal research terminology is confined to private tools and engineering documentation.

## Visual and interaction rules

Use quiet obsidian surfaces and controlled coral/crimson action color. Preserve legible Latin typography and correct Arabic directionality. Use Egyptian architectural geometry purposefully and celestial motifs sparingly. Remove fireworks, moving tickers, repeated ornaments, and repeated portrait-centered compositions. Motion follows an interaction and respects reduced-motion preferences. Every journey supports keyboard, touch, visible focus, and readable loading/error states.

The existing masked portrait is an altered owner-supplied design exploration, not an approved documentary photograph. Preserve its provenance and show it only in this undeployed review context with an adjacent qualification. Public release requires an original creator-approved asset and authorization for likeness, name, signature, and logo. Do not generate an uncovered face or third-party anime insignia. Home may use one portrait; About may use a contextual crop. Other pages do not repeat it.

Unreviewed Qur'an fixtures do not render publicly. Future Arabic text must preserve exact orthography, correct RTL, source edition, reference, translation license, and qualified editorial review. Sacred text is never distorted or animated.

## Accounts, community, and money

Browser demo state is not an account. Real accounts use verified Supabase sessions; authorization comes from trusted server-managed roles. Account data is private by default and exportable/deletable. Typed handles are self-entered, never proof of identity or attendance. Community publication requires moderation readiness.

XP is a non-purchasable participation measure, never religious merit. Optional monetary credits are a separate, inactive architecture. Basic guidance remains free. No billing activation, Stripe products, deployment, marketing messages, or paid inference is authorized by this mission.
