# Optional support architecture — inactive

The foundation and basic religious guidance remain free. No paid tier, price, checkout, Stripe product, webhook deployment or payment credential is activated by this branch.

XP means non-purchasable participation only. It cannot represent faith, piety, conversion success, religious standing, or purchase priority in dialogue. Monetary credits, if later approved, require a separate ledger, transparent unit pricing, expiration/refund terms, and clearly identified optional original services. Buying credits cannot increase XP or purchase spiritual ranking. YouTube playback cannot become the sold service.

Future Supporter/Patron experiences require creator approval, actual delivered benefits, moderation capacity, legal/privacy review and an explicit activation decision. Transactions must use server-verified signed webhooks, idempotent event IDs, immutable adjustments, rate limits and reconciliation. No client may mint ledger credits, trusted attendance or earned XP.

A non-public `pricing-proposal.md` is retained in `/workspace/itachi-private-docs/` for this review session. It contains no authorized prices and must move to the creator's chosen private documentation destination, not this public repository. Public Support gives honest readiness information and approved external destinations only.
