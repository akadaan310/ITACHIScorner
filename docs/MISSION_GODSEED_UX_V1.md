# THE DESIGN DNA — EGYPT BY NIGHT, DA'WAH AMONG THE STARS

- **Egyptian heart:** Cairo's Mamluk and Fatimid architectural geometry, arabesque mashrabiyya screenwork, intricate 8/10/12-point stars, luminous Cairo-night architecture, parchment and carved stone, textured Nile-blue ceramics, brass lanterns, muted desert dunes and warm Nile reflections. No franchise copying, no comic-book hypersexualization, no generic FPS weapon imagery.

No spurious quotation art, invented ahadith, or animation that deforms sacred script. Keep abstract patterns behind/around text rather than overprinting the Qur'an.
- **Human elegance:** inviting copy, beautiful portrait treatment when approved, comfortable reading density, tasteful negative space. This is graceful, clever, and vibrant—never tacky neon cyberpunk spam.
