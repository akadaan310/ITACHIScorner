# Research limitations and release boundaries

At the foundation handoff, this repository has no authorized real corpus: **0 verified imported videos, 0 imported real transcript versions and 0 validated research findings**. An empty discovery registry is intentional. No measured creator-specific recurrence, coverage, ASR robustness or pedagogical effectiveness can be reported yet.

## What the baseline can establish

Given an authorized export, it can validate a manifest/track contract, preserve exact-byte checksums and short half-open intervals, import immutable source versions, produce deterministic heuristic features, count structural transitions and contiguous motifs, evaluate held-out sequence baselines, run specified seeded controls and track dependency staleness. Synthetic test fixtures establish only those software behaviors.

## What remains unestablished

- **Inventory completeness:** a supplied manifest is not proof that every canonical channel video, playlist, short, archive, private item or deleted item was discovered. Reconciliation against the corpus owner's permitted inventory is still required.
- **Rights and provenance:** a URL, local file or matching checksum does not establish acquisition permission, creator consent, licensing or authenticity. No authentication bypass, logged-in scraping or transcript publication is authorized.
- **Independent support:** selected tracks avoid duplicate counting within a video, and `recording_id` can group derived clips. Incorrect or missing recording lineage still causes pseudoreplication. Separate videos are not necessarily independent conversations.
- **Language and roles:** script-aware tokens are not reliable language identification, Arabic morphology, code-switch analysis, diarization or personal identity recognition. Roles are supplied annotations. Speech-act rules produce fallible proposals that require manual assessment.
- **Semantic structure:** question marks, citation cues and discourse markers cannot reliably identify argument claims, quotations, scope, interruptions, intent, source correctness or theological interpretation. The implementation is not an ISNAAD detector or a Qur'anic attribution model. A future ISNAAD adapter must be explicitly versioned and separately validated.
- **Pattern expressiveness:** bounded contiguous motifs and a first-order Markov model miss long-range returns, gapped dependencies and rich argument graphs. Counts are descriptive; the implementation does not claim grammar induction, PrefixSpan/SPADE, community detection or causal discovery.
- **Discrimination:** no authorized matched dialogue comparison set exists. Topic, guest type, video length, moderation and annotation confounders remain uncontrolled. A recurrent pattern is not thereby unique to Itachi.
- **Evaluation:** chronological held-out recordings reduce some leakage but do not establish generalization across all future guests or topics. Insufficient dated independent groups make estimates unavailable. Bootstrap uncertainty excludes systematic transcription and sampling bias.
- **Perturbations:** shuffles are frequency-preserving null models, role swaps test role dependence, and citation ablation tests citation dependence. Token deletion tests one simplified ASR perturbation only. Their results are not interchangeable and must not share a blanket pass/fail rule.
- **Falsification:** counterexamples concern the declared antecedent/suffix prediction. Unknown or truncated opportunities are censored. A surviving motif is not a proven teaching mechanism or causal intervention.
- **Operational integrations:** private local artifacts do not prove remote Supabase persistence, reviewer authentication, storage policy enforcement, corpus acquisition or working timestamp destinations. These need separate configured verification.

## Human and publication gates

Every analysis finding remains a pending proposal with `pedagogy_eligible: false`. An approval-shaped JSON object is not authoritative reviewer identity or creator consent. Stale evidence invalidates downstream review until revalidation. Real source spans, correction lineage, independent corroboration, explicit gaps, counterexamples, uncertainty, qualified editorial assessment and creator authorization must precede attribution to Itachi. Theological assertions also require appropriate scholarly review.

No substantive Itachi curriculum, lesson, practice scenario, technique taxonomy or book text is generated by this baseline. Learning dependency and editorial schemas are scaffolding. Public teaching and publication remain blocked until Gates 3–4 pass. General resources require their own verified attribution and review; they cannot be relabeled as observations of Itachi.

No metric may measure religious truth, piety, divine approval, debate victory or conversion success. Dialogue statistics cannot prove scripture or establish that an observed conversion report was caused by a technique.

## Work deliberately deferred

Licensed corpus reconciliation, timestamp/source spot-checks, manual annotation quality studies, multilingual source verification, independent contrast dialogues, more expressive sequence/argument models, reviewer-authenticated promotion and downstream pedagogy are future work. Optional LLM proposals remain secondary to exact source evidence. There is no deployment, live billing, paid inference or Modal spend in this foundation.
